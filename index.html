Questions

The things bands actually ask

We have never been filmed. Does that matter?

Not at all. 70's Child had never stood in front of a camera either. Clear photographs of each member from a few angles are enough for us to build them into the video.

Do we pay anything before we see results?

No. The concept and the first test scene are free. You pay once the finished video is approved by you, and not a day earlier.

How long does it take?

A full video usually takes about a week from the moment we have the song and the photos. A rush turnaround is possible if you have a release date.

Who owns the video?

You do, completely. You can monetise it, license it, run it as an ad or send it to a label. We only ask to show it in our portfolio, and even that is optional.

What if we do not like the first version?

Then we change it. Revisions are unlimited on the full packages, and we keep going until you are genuinely happy with the result.

What genres do you work with?

All of them. Rock, country, metal, blues, hip-hop, folk. What matters is that the song means something to you - that is what gives us a story to build around.
